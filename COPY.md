## Frequently asked questions

**May I bring my children?**  
Please check with Roger or Janine before including children in your plans. We’ll confirm whether they are included in your invitation so we can arrange seating with care.

**May I bring a plus-one?**  
Your RSVP form will show the number of additional guests included in your invitation. Please respond only for the places reserved for you. If no additional place appears, please contact us before making plans for a companion.

**Where can I park?**  
We’re confirming the parking arrangements with Kabaleyan Cove Resort. Please contact Roger or Janine before the day if you need parking or drop-off guidance.

**How do I RSVP?**  
Enter your first and last name as written on your invitation, together with your private invitation code. Select attending or declining for yourself and each companion included, then choose “Send our response.” A confirmation will appear once your response is saved.

**When should I respond?**  
The RSVP deadline will be announced here once confirmed. Thank you for helping us plan a warm welcome for everyone.

**Can I change my response?**  
Yes. While RSVPs are open, use the same name and invitation code to review and update your response. If RSVPs have closed, please contact Roger or Janine directly.

**What if my name isn’t found?**  
Please check the spelling on your invitation and try again. If you still need help, contact Roger or Janine through the number or message thread used for your invitation.
